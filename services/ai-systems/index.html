What it actually is

Not a chatbot bolted onto your homepage. These are systems that run in the background of the business and plug into the tools you already use: your calendar, your inbox, your phone, your invoicing, your customer list.

Every build starts the same way. We find the one job that is costing you the most, build the system that removes it, and only then look at the next one. One thing working beats five things half wired up.

Questions people actually ask

What kind of AI do you actually build?

Whatever the business needs and can measure. Most often that is follow-up and review requests, reactivating past customers, quoting, intake and scheduling, and back-office admin. If a job is repetitive, rule-based and currently costing you hours or leads, it is a candidate.

Is this a chatbot on my website?

No. These systems run in the background and connect to your calendar, inbox, phone and customer list. A chat widget is one small option, not the product.

Do I need a website from you first?

No. An AI system layers onto any existing site, or onto a business with no site at all.

Can it answer my phone?

Yes, if that is genuinely your biggest leak. It is one of the things a system can do, set up around your business rather than sold as a standalone product.

What does it cost?

From $399 per month, billed month to month with no long-term contract. The exact number depends on what gets built and what it connects to, and you get it in writing before anything starts.

What if it does not work for my business?

Then I will tell you that on the first call. Plenty of small businesses do not have a problem AI solves yet, and selling you one anyway would waste both our time.

Tell me what keeps costing you.

One call. If there is a system worth building, you will know what it is and roughly what it costs before we hang up.
