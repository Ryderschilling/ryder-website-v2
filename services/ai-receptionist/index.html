The problem it fixes

Most local businesses lose more work to an unanswered phone than to any competitor. The call comes in while you are on a job, on a ladder, with a customer, or asleep. The caller does not leave a voicemail. They call the next business on the list.

The AI receptionist picks up every one of those calls. It speaks in your business's voice, knows your services, your hours and your service area, and it books the job rather than taking a message.

What it does on a call

  • 01

    Answers instantly

    Every call, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays. No hold music, no queue.

  • 02

    Answers real questions

    Pricing ranges, service area, hours, what you do and do not do, how soon you can get out there.

  • 03

    Qualifies the caller

    Finds out what they need, where they are, and whether they are actually a fit before it takes up your time.

  • 04

    Books the appointment

    Writes the job straight onto your calendar in an open slot, then confirms it with the caller.

  • 05

    Texts you a summary

    Who called, what they wanted, what was booked. You see it the moment you put the tools down.

  • 06

    Hands off when it should

    Anything it cannot or should not handle gets routed to you as a live transfer or a flagged callback.
