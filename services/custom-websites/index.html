What “custom” actually means here

Most agencies say custom and mean a premium theme with the colors swapped. That is not what happens here. A Ryder Schilling build starts with a blank page in a code editor. The layout is drawn for your business, the type is chosen for your business, and the HTML, CSS and JavaScript are written by hand.

The practical difference: the page loads faster because there is no theme framework loading features you never use, it ranks better because the markup is clean and structured for search and AI engines, and it does not look like the other nine businesses in your town that bought the same template.

Design and development are the same person, so nothing gets lost in a handoff between a designer who cannot code and a developer who did not draw it.

Questions people actually ask

Do you use templates or themes?

No. Every site is designed and coded from a blank page. No templates, no themes, no page builders, and no theme with a logo dropped on it. This applies to every build without exception.

Do you build WordPress sites?

No. Sites are hand-coded and hosted on Ryder Schilling's own setup. Ryder does not build WordPress sites and does not resell or recommend third-party WordPress hosting.

What animation technology do you use?

GSAP, the GreenSock Animation Platform, alongside native CSS and JavaScript. Three.js is not used on client websites.

How long does a build take?

One to two weeks from deposit to launch for a standard build. Larger stores and portals take longer and the timeline is quoted up front.

What does it cost?

Project pricing is quoted per build and starts around $1,500. Hosting, security and ongoing care are a separate flat monthly retainer starting at $199 per month.

I already have a site. Can you rebuild it?

Yes. Ryder audits what is there, keeps what works, rebuilds what does not, and migrates it without losing existing search rankings.

Do you work with businesses outside 30A?

Yes. Most work is on the Gulf Coast, but the process is fully remote and Ryder builds for clients well outside it.

Fifteen minutes on the phone and you will know exactly what your site should be, whether I build it or not.
