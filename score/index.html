What the check actually looks at

Fourteen checks, weighted, scored out of 100. No vanity metrics, only the things that decide whether a visitor stays and whether Google sends you any in the first place.

  • SpeedHow fast your server answers and how heavy the page is before a single image loads. Slow pages lose people before they read a word.
  • MobileWhether the page is genuinely built for a phone or just shrunk down. Most of your traffic is on a phone.
  • TrustHTTPS, the padlock, and the basics that stop a browser warning people away from you.
  • How Google reads youTitle, description, headings, structured data. The difference between being findable and being invisible.
  • Contact pathsWhether a visitor who wants to hire you can find a phone number, an address, or a form without hunting.

Questions people actually ask

Is this really free?

Yes. The score, the grade and the verdict cost nothing and need no email. The itemised report is unlocked with a name and an email so I know who I am talking to.

How long does it take?

About ten seconds. The scanner fetches your live page and runs the checks in real time, it is not pulling a cached report from somewhere else.

Do you store my site or my results?

Not for the score. Nothing is saved. If you unlock the full report, your name, email and result come to my inbox so I can follow up.

My score is low. Does that mean I need a whole new site?

Often no. A lot of what gets flagged is a fix, not a rebuild. I will tell you plainly which one you are looking at, even when the honest answer is that you do not need me.

Will this work on any website?

Any public site that is live and reachable. Pages behind a login, a password, or an aggressive firewall cannot be fetched.
