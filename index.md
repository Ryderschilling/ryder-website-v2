## Frequently asked questions

**Why a custom site instead of a template?**
Templates get you online. Custom gets you chosen. Your site is shaped around your offer,
your customers, and how you actually sell, so it loads faster, ranks better, and feels like
nobody else's.

**Is it worth the cost, and what am I actually getting?**
You're getting a site designed and coded from a blank page for your business: the copy and
page structure, the SEO groundwork, and whatever features you need built in (booking,
payments, a store, a portal, an AI receptionist). Not a theme with your logo dropped on it.
It pays for itself when it turns visitors you're already getting into work you're currently
losing.

**Do you host it, or do I have to figure that out?**
I host it. You never touch a server, a registrar, or a control panel. Once you approve the
build I put it live on my setup and keep it fast, secure, and online for a flat monthly fee.
If something breaks, you call me, not a support queue in another time zone.

**Do you handle design, or only development?**
Both. Most projects start as a blank page and leave as a finished brand experience: layout,
type, color, motion, and the code underneath it all.

**Already have a site that needs work?**
Happens all the time. I audit what's there, keep what works, rebuild what doesn't, and
migrate everything without losing your rankings or your mind.

**What happens after launch?**
I host it and keep it live. Power-Ups cover light monthly care, and the AI Upgrade is the
recurring layer: a receptionist that answers 24/7, follow-ups that bring customers back, and
automations that kill busywork.

**What's the process from start to launch?**
You tell me what your business needs, you pay to get on the schedule, then I design and code
it. You see progress the whole way, and when it's ready you review it and tell me what to
change. Nothing goes live until you approve it. After launch I host it and keep it running,
billed monthly.

**How do revisions and feedback work?**
Simple: you point, I polish. Feedback rounds are built into every project, and small tweaks
after launch are usually same-day.

**Do you work with businesses outside 30A?**
Yes. Most of my work is on the Gulf Coast, but the process is fully remote and I build for
clients well outside it.

**Not sure which option fits your project?**
Call me. Fifteen minutes, no pitch. You'll leave knowing exactly what your site needs,
whether we build it together or not.
