## What was actually missing

Pages audited: 1 (`/`), scoring 69.

- **No structured data at all.** Zero JSON-LD on the page. AI engines were guessing what
  the site is. (Action ID SCH-01, P0.)
- **No `llms.txt`.** No AI-readable description of the business. (ROOT-01, P1.)
- **Business identity incomplete.** No business name, address, city, state, postal code,
  or linked profiles anywhere a machine could read. Without a name, external directories
  could not even be probed for citations. (GEO-01, P1.)
- **No `robots.txt`.** All bots technically allowed, but most AI crawlers look for the
  file first. (ROOT-02.)
- **No `llms-full.txt`.** (GEO-02.)
- **No reachable sitemap.** (GEO-03.)
- **No canonical URL.** Engines could index or cite the wrong URL. (SEO-01.)
- **No author signal.** No `meta name="author"` and no Person schema. (SCH-02.)
- **Page reads like an FAQ but has no FAQPage schema** — 10 real Q&As invisible as
  answers. (SCH-03.)
- **No Markdown twin of the page.** (SEO-02.)
- **No Open Graph tags.** Links unfurled broken or blank. (SEO-03.)
- **No Twitter/X card tags.** (SEO-04.)
- **Two `<h1>` tags** (desktop hero + mobile hero), exactly 1 expected. (STR-01.)
- **Video embedded with no VideoObject schema** — the reel was invisible to AI. (SCH-04.)

## Security findings (baseline)

- HIGH — no Content-Security-Policy.
- MEDIUM — no X-Frame-Options and no CSP `frame-ancestors` (clickjackable).
- MEDIUM — no `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`.
- LOW — no Referrer-Policy.
- LOW — no Permissions-Policy.
- LOW — no DKIM key at common selectors (informational; a custom selector may exist).
- ADVISORY — DMARC is enforcing at `p=quarantine`; every sending tool needs to be
  confirmed authorised or its mail gets quarantined.

Already passing: HSTS, HTTPS everywhere, HTTP→HTTPS redirect, no mixed content, server
version hidden, CORS not open, no secrets in client code, `.env` and `.git` not exposed,
no source maps, no vulnerable JS libraries, SRI on third-party scripts, no directory
listing, no stack traces, forms submit securely, no leftover template content.
